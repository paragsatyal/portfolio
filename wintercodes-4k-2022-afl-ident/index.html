Wintercodes 4K 2022 AFL Ident

An ident made to promo Foxtel 4K UHD channel. The shoe was 3D scanned from an actual shoe and then some additional texturing done in Substance Painter as the shoe needed to look used. All textures needed to made in 8K because the render was being done in 4K and 50 frames per second.

Art Directors: Radge Ratsaphong, Tim Buhagiar
Design Director: Sally-Anne Kerr
3D Artists: Parag Satyal and Grischa Theissen

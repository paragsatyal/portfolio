Photography

Delta400 in EI1600  + Microphen 

Tried shooting a roll of Delta400 Professional. It's supposed to be finer grain than HP5+ but I don't really see too much difference between Delta400 and HP5+ when developed with Microphen, I might continue using HP5+ in future. 

 

HP5+ in EI1600  + Microphen 

My first time pushing HP5+ film to 1600 (base speed 400). This was also the first time using Microphen developer, which is allegedly better developer to push film with. This is has enabled to me to try shooting at night without flash (although I do love flash photography, it's good to have options). The only issue is I need to carry around an ND filter if I want to shoot out in midday.

First batch of HP5+ on Olympus OM1

I order myself an OM1 from eBay and jump headfirst into a new hobby. I'd always been a digital photographer and had shot here film a few times over the years but this time I was going to learn to develop rolls myself. To me, that's the only way it makes to be shooting on film, if I develop them myself. A future plan is to learn how to darkroom print at home as well.
